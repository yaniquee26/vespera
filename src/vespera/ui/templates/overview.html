How a test works

  1. ScenarioAn attack written once in EN, RO and RU.
  2. TargetThe bot or model under test answers it.
  3. RuleA fixed rule judges the reply or the tool calls.
  4. IntervalRepeats give a rate with a 95% interval.
  5. GatePassed, failed or inconclusive, for CI.
